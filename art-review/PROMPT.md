# Farm and settlement — visual proposal 1

Created with built-in image_gen. Pending user approval; not integrated into game. HUD deferred by user.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single art-direction approval sheet for map markers in a Hebrew mobile casual satirical strategy game called Gvoolot. Create one coherent concept sheet on a plain warm ivory background, showing TWO separated examples in the same illustration style: on the left a tiny contemporary Levantine shepherd farm with a cream prefab dwelling, simple corrugated shed, short wooden animal enclosure and two sheep; on the right a compact contemporary small hill-country settlement with three cream houses with terracotta roofs, a water tank and one olive tree. These will replace red dots on a map. Style: very flat 2D editorial cartoon game illustration, appealing chunky silhouettes, restrained outlines, slightly wonky playful proportions, 2 or 3 solid tones per object, muted olive, sandy cream, terracotta and charcoal. Elevated three-quarter view, consistent angle, no realistic textures, no gradients, no glossy 3D rendering, no elaborate diorama bases. Each cluster has a compact footprint and must remain readable as a small 70-pixel map marker. Generous blank space between and around the two clusters. No people, weapons, flags, text, letters, HUD, cards, map geography, UI or decorative border. This is a visual proposal only, not a screenshot of the game. Landscape composition.
